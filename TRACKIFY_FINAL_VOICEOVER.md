# TRACKIFY Demo Voice-Over Script (Final & Condensed)
## Single Person Narration — 2:00–2:30 minutes
## Video Recording — Project Explanation

---

## **[00:00–00:08] Intro**

Hello, this is a video recording about TRACKIFY project explanation. We are the TRACKIFY team. TRACKIFY is a secure Visitor Management and Staff Tracking system that streamlines campus entry, notifications, and reporting. Important: TRACKIFY records all times automatically — visitors never manually enter in-time or out-time.

---

## **[00:08–00:50] VISITOR FLOW (Registration → Checkout)**

Both visitors and staff use the same QR code. A new visitor enters phone, name, email, and place. They receive an OTP via email for verification. After OTP, they fill visit purpose and whom to meet, then click "Submit." The website closes immediately to prevent spam and fraud — visitors cannot fake checkout from outside campus without re-scanning.

A returning visitor skips registration, just enters phone and visit details, then submits. Website closes for the same reason.

To checkout, the visitor scans the QR again, enters phone, and clicks "Check-out." Website closes. In-time is recorded automatically when receptionist accepts the request. Out-time is recorded automatically when the visitor clicks checkout.

---

## **[00:50–01:10] RECEPTIONIST Dashboard**

Receptionist logs in with phone and password. They review pending visitor requests on the dashboard. Click "Accept" — an email is sent to the visitor confirming approval, and an email is sent to the host staff member only. Click "Reject" — an email is sent to the visitor.

---

## **[01:10–01:28] STAFF FLOW (Exit & Entry)**

Staff scan the QR and enter phone number. To go out, they enter a purpose and click "Record Exit." The website closes to prevent fraud. Later, they scan the QR again, enter phone, and click "Record Entry." Website closes. Both entry and exit times are recorded automatically.

---

## **[01:28–01:40] SECURITY Dashboard**

Security logs in and views total visitors, currently inside, staff out, and staff in. The visitor table shows color-coded status: green (accepted), yellow (pending), red (rejected). This allows security to verify visitor requests are properly registered in the system.

---

## **[01:40–01:52] PRINCIPAL Dashboard**

Principal logs in and generates today's or custom-date reports with numbered visitor and staff details. Download reports as CSV or PDF for audits.

---

## **[01:52–02:00] Tech Stack**

Built with Node.js, Express, MySQL, OTP verification, JWT, bcrypt hashing, and parameterized queries. Website closing prevents fraud and ensures security.

---

## **[02:00–02:05] Closing**

TRACKIFY makes campus entry safer, faster, and auditable. Thank you.

---
